# Tutorial — How to Play (Prototype)

Overview
- This is a simplified, browser-playable prototype of "Back in Time — Chapter 2." The goal is to move through rooms, find puzzle hotspots, solve the puzzles you choose, and survive encounters with the monster.

Flow
- Walk to a puzzle hotspot (a glowing circle). Press F to interact and open the puzzle UI.
- When you complete a puzzle it fades (becomes inactive). You can then move to other puzzles.
- Complete all puzzles in a room to unlock the next area.

Puzzles in this prototype
- Simon Says: Repeat a color sequence.
- Drag Cables: Drag cable endpoints to their matching sockets.
- Bomb Disable: A short timed step puzzle to stop a bomb.

Survival
- A roaming monster chases you. Colliding with it reduces health.
- Use the flashlight (Space) to stun the monster for a few seconds.
- Health bar is shown in the top-left. If it reaches 0, you are reset to the room start.

Controls and tips are in CONTROLS.md. See DESIGN.md for puzzle design notes.
