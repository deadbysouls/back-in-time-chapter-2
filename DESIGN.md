Design notes — Chapter 2 (prototype)

Puzzle behaviour
- Puzzles are placed as hotspots in rooms. The player walks to any hotspot and presses F to start the puzzle.
- Solving a puzzle marks it completed and it visually fades and becomes unusable.
- Puzzles vary in difficulty and required steps; none are instant. Some have timers, some need memory or dexterity.

Puzzle types included for the prototype
- Simon Says: memory-based sequence of colors
- Drag Cables: match wires to sockets by dragging
- Bomb Disable: short timer + code sequence

Gameplay features
- Health bar and monster AI: monster chases the player; flashlight stuns it.
- Sprint (Shift): faster movement for quick repositioning.
