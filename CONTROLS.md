Controls

- Move: WASD or Arrow keys
- Sprint: Hold Shift (increases speed while held)
- Interact / Use: F (when near a puzzle hotspot)
- Flashlight / Stun: Space (stuns the monster when it is in range)
- Pause: Esc (closes puzzle or overlay)

Notes
- Sprint drains no stamina in prototype — it only increases movement speed.
- Flashlight has a short cooldown (3 seconds) in this prototype.
